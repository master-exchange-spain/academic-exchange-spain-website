Our Story

Designed around each university.

Academic Exchange Spain was created with a clear purpose: to help universities develop meaningful academic experiences in Spain through personalized planning, trusted local partnerships and careful coordination.

Rather than offering standard itineraries, we work alongside each institution to create programs that reflect its curriculum, learning outcomes and international strategy. Our role is simple: to become your local academic partner in Spain.

Every University Is Different. Every Program Should Be Too.

No two universities share the same objectives, academic culture or student profile. For this reason, we believe international education should never begin with a predefined package.

Instead, we take the time to understand your institution before designing an experience that supports your academic goals. This philosophy guides every recommendation we make and every partnership we build.

What We Do

We collaborate with universities to design faculty-led programs, customized academic programs, Spanish language courses, academic and industry visits, student accommodation, cultural experiences and local coordination. Every solution is designed to support your institution from initial planning through program delivery.
