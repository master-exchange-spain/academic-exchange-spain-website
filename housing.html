Housing That Supports the Academic Experience

For universities sending students abroad, housing is one of the most important parts of program design. The right accommodation supports safety, comfort, community and access to academic activities.

Academic Exchange Spain helps universities identify accommodation options that fit the program duration, city, student profile, budget and level of support required.

Accommodation Options

University Residences

Ideal for groups seeking structure, community and convenient access to academic activities. Residences can support orientation, group cohesion and daily student life.

Homestays

Designed for programs where language immersion and cultural connection are a priority. Homestays can help students practice Spanish and experience local life more closely.

Shared Apartments

Suitable for longer programs and students who require more independence while remaining connected to the academic experience.

Hotels for Faculty and Executive Groups

Appropriate for short-term programs, faculty leaders, executive education and professional groups.

Our Housing Process

We support universities with needs assessment, accommodation selection, reservation coordination, arrival planning, check-in guidance, local support and departure coordination. The objective is to reduce uncertainty and help students feel supported from the beginning of the program.

Student Wellbeing

Wellbeing begins where students live.

Accommodation is part of the learning environment. A well-selected housing solution helps students rest, connect, adapt and focus on the academic goals of the program.

We prioritize safe, appropriate and program-aligned housing options for each university partner.
